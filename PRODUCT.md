# Internet Mint Finder

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Cashu wallet users finding a mint, reading public reviews and contributing their
experience with a Nostr signing extension. Phase one is the directory and reviews;
phase two displays results from an independently operated auditor.

## Existing identity

Preserve the shipped light/dark interface, holographic IMF tag, typography,
directory rows, mint information cards and review composition. The existing
`.impeccable/design.json` and `style/main.css` are the visual references.

## Principles

- Reading is public; signing is an explicit action in the user's extension.
- Show ratings as community experience, never a guarantee about a mint.
- Preserve keyboard focus, accessible form labels, responsive layouts and reduced motion.
- Keep deployment and protocol implementation details out of the browsing flow.

## Anti-references

Do not replace the established interface with a generic dashboard or introduce
private-key entry fields. No redesign is part of this rescope.
